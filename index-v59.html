ABBridge Accounting

Accounting support for your business

From day-to-day bookkeeping to annual accounts and tax responsibilities, our services are designed for sole traders, freelancers and small companies.

How we can help

Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping keeps a record of the money coming into and leaving your business. We organise income, expenses and financial records so that your accounts remain up to date and ready when required.

Suitable for: sole traders, freelancers and small companies.

Self Assessment

Self Assessment is the process used by many self-employed people to report their income and calculate the tax they owe. We prepare and file your tax return using the financial information you provide.

Suitable for: sole traders, freelancers and individuals with additional income.

Limited company accounts

Limited companies must prepare annual accounts and meet Corporation Tax responsibilities. We organise the necessary financial information, prepare the accounts and help the company meet its reporting obligations.

Suitable for: directors and small limited companies.

Payroll

Payroll covers employee wages, payslips, deductions and related records. We help calculate staff pay and prepare the information required for each payroll period.

Suitable for: businesses employing one or more people.

VAT

VAT-registered businesses need to maintain suitable records, calculate VAT and report the relevant figures. We help organise the records and prepare the required VAT information.

Suitable for: VAT-registered sole traders and companies.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need bookkeeping or accounting support?

Bookkeeping keeps your day-to-day financial records organised. Accounting uses those records to prepare accounts, calculate tax and meet reporting responsibilities. Some businesses require both.

Do you work with sole traders?

Yes. We support sole traders with bookkeeping, Self Assessment and other relevant accounting responsibilities.

What information will I need to provide?

This depends on the service, but it may include invoices, receipts, bank statements, payroll information and previous accounts. We explain what is required before beginning.

How are fees calculated?

The fee depends on the services required and the complexity of the work. The work and cost are agreed before we begin.

Can I ask for help if I do not know which service I need?

Yes. An introductory consultation can help identify the most appropriate starting point.
